Student Commuters

Practical reminders for students travelling to and from school.

Student travel checklist

Students may commute early in the morning, during busy periods, or after school activities. Planning ahead and following basic safety practices can help make trips more manageable.

  • Check your class schedule and allow enough travel time.
  • Prepare your fare or payment method before boarding.
  • Keep your school bag close and avoid blocking aisles.
  • Use designated pedestrian crossings and follow traffic signals.
  • Tell a trusted person if your travel plan changes.

Budget and time awareness

Track your usual transport expenses and check current fares from official or operator sources. Do not rely on outdated fare information.
