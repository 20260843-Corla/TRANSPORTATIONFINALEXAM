Ideas for Better Commuting

Explore community and city-level ideas that can support more inclusive journeys.

Ideas worth supporting

Improving commuting takes cooperation among government agencies, transport operators, communities, planners, and passengers. Solutions should respond to local needs and be evaluated over time.

  • Improve safe and continuous sidewalks and pedestrian crossings.
  • Design stops and terminals that consider different accessibility needs.
  • Share clear, updated, and easy-to-understand travel information.
  • Encourage responsible public transport use and sustainable travel choices.
  • Collect community feedback and use it to identify recurring problems.

Connect to SDG 11

Sustainable Development Goal 11 calls for cities and communities to be inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable. Accessible transport is one important part of that broader goal.
